The company behind Delta Oil and Delta Plus

DSF Trading owns and brings together two distinct businesses in Egypt. Delta Oil works in used cooking oil collection and supply for the biodiesel industry. Delta Plus is a wholesale marketplace serving restaurants, hotels and other food businesses.

Delta Oil services

Collection that makes participation easier

Delta Oil collects used cooking oil through several channels. These service models help households and food businesses take part.

01 / MOBILE COLLECTION

Collection near households

Local collectors can arrange a pickup and bring collection closer to residents.

02 / LOCAL DROP-OFF

Fixed collection points

Neighbourhood shops can offer a convenient place for households to hand over used cooking oil.

03 / FOOD BUSINESSES

Hospitality collection

Restaurants, cafés and hotels can direct their waste oil toward a more responsible end use.

Quality.
Quality

Attention to the material, from collection onward

Reliable biodiesel feedstock depends on consistent handling and quality checks. Care at each step helps collected oil meet the needs of its next use.

We value clear processes, responsible practices and continued improvement throughout the supply chain.

Circular economy

Keeping materials in productive use can reduce waste and the need to discard valuable resources.

Biodiesel

Used cooking oil can serve as a renewable input to biodiesel production.

Sustainability
